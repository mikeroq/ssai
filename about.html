Our story

We didn't invent SSaI.
We just named it.

The pattern was already there. In every finance team. In every ops backroom. In every startup that "just needs to track one thing." We are simply the first company brave enough to put a logo on it.

Origin story

In the spring of 2022, our founder Naomi Kessler walked into a Series B startup whose entire billing system was a single Google Sheet named final_pricing_v4_REAL.xlsx. Three engineers maintained it. One had been there nine months without ever opening a code editor. Revenue: $14M ARR.

"This isn't a workaround," Naomi reportedly said. "This is the architecture."

SSaI was incorporated four weeks later — coincidentally as a Google Sheet shared with our first three employees, the CFO of the original startup, and Naomi's mother (who has editor access to this day, and remains our most reliable QA tester).

Manifesto

We believe infrastructure should be visible. We believe a row is a record. We believe the most powerful database in the world is the one your CFO can open without filing a ticket. We believe a green cell is a passing test. We believe in the link, and the link only. We believe migrations are a personal choice. We believe in shipping.

Values

  • The grid is the contract. If it's not in a cell, it doesn't exist.
  • Bias toward formatting. Every problem looks smaller in conditional yellow.
  • Trust, but tab. Audit trails are sheet names.
  • Don't fix it 'til row 49,997. Premature optimization is moving to Postgres.
  • Be the spreadsheet you wish to see in the world.
Milestones

Four years of regret, monetized.

MAR 2022 — Q1

SSaI is incorporated inside a spreadsheet.

The cap table is also the company. Cell B7 is a 4% advisor grant. Cell B8 is "TBD (legal)".

SEP 2022 — Q3

First customer: a fintech with no engineers.

They processed $2.1M in transactions through `IMPORTRANGE`. The auditor never recovered.

JUN 2023 — Q2

Seed round closed via shared link.

Lead investor edited cell C14 to update valuation. Round considered "signed" once the green dot disappeared.

JAN 2024 — Q1

Launched the SSaI Whitepaper.

A 47-page academic treatise on putting `=VLOOKUP()` in production. Read it here.

MAR 2025 — Q1

Reached 1,000,000 cells under management.

We celebrated by archiving 38% of them and pretending it didn't happen.

NOV 2025 — Q4

SSaI is featured in HBR as "alarming."

We added it to the press page. See coverage.
