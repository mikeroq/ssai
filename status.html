Uptime philosophy

SSaI does not compute uptime in the traditional sense. We define "up" as: at least one editor is able to load the primary sheet within 10 seconds. We define "down" as: nobody can.

By this definition, our trailing-90-day uptime is 99.91%. The 0.09% downtime corresponds to the moment Google Sheets had a regional outage in 2024 and we, like everyone else, had to wait it out.

We commit to no SLA. We commit only to genuine attention.
