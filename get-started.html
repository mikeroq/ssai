Three steps

From zero to production in 90 seconds.

Most customers complete the onboarding before they realize they've started it.

1

Open a blank sheet

Visit sheets.new. Name your spreadsheet after the business problem you intend to ignore.

2

Add headers in row 1

Your headers are your schema. Choose them carefully. Or don't. You can always rename them later (live, in production).

3

Share the link

Set permissions to "anyone with the link → editor." This is your auth system. You are now in production.

What comes next

Once you've opened your sheet, we recommend the following sequence:

  1. Read the eight tenets. Pin the page if you must.
  2. Adopt Cell-Driven Design for any logic you intend to ship.
  3. Establish a Conditional Formatting as Code review process before your team grows past three.
  4. Bookmark our status page for moments of doubt.
  5. Read the migration journey so you know where you are when you get there.

And if at any point you have questions: just DM the founder.
