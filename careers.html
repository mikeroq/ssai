Our hiring process

  1. Apply. Reply to the role with a link to a Google Sheet that you are proud of.
  2. Sheet review. A panel of 3 SSaI engineers evaluates your formula technique, tab discipline, and font choices.
  3. Live edit. 60-minute paired session in a shared sheet. We watch your cursor. We are not nervous, you are.
  4. Values interview. One conversation about a time you almost moved to Postgres, and what stopped you.
  5. Reference check. We talk to one person who has ever shared a sheet with you.
  6. Offer. Delivered as a comment on cell A1 of Hiring_Pipeline_v12.

End-to-end: roughly 7–10 days, depending on how many tabs you have open.
