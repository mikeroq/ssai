Is SSaI a real product?

It is as real as the moment your finance team unilaterally became your back-end engineering team. SSaI describes the most widely-deployed architecture in modern business — we just gave it a name and a logo.

How does SSaI handle concurrency?

Through interpersonal trust and a clearly worded Slack message: "please don't touch the sheet rn 🙏". Locks are advisory. Outcomes are spiritual.

What about compliance?

Spreadsheets pre-date most modern compliance frameworks. We argue this gives SSaI grandfather rights. Counsel disagrees.

Does SSaI scale?

Yes — until row 50,001. At which point you split the sheet. See Tenet VIII: Horizontal scaling is two spreadsheets.

Can I migrate off SSaI?

Eventually, painfully, and with the help of a very expensive consultant. This is included in the journey. See phase 4.
